Q1 · How is the theme settings window opened from a document window?

Main window 800 × 460 (tree 230 px), shown at 54%; details at 120%. Every lane keeps Ctrl+, (Cmd+, on macOS), and one settings window at a time: a second request brings the open one forward. The viewer row is a viewer window (one document, no tree), which shares the title bar.

Q2 · The open theme's file changed on disk while the window holds unsaved edits

Ruled: the edits stay, and a bar says "This theme changed on disk" with Reload (drops your edits, loads the file) and Keep mine (keeps editing; your next save overwrites the file). With no unsaved edits the window reloads silently. Save patches the file's text to match the working copy, so after Keep mine it also puts back every token the outside edit changed. The settings window is 980 × 640, shown at 68%, editing Night Owl (Yours) with two unsaved tokens while the app uses Studio; the bar spans the window under its title bar, above the three columns. The detail rows draw the bars alone at the window's 760 px minimum width.

Q3 · The theme in use is deleted (or renamed) outside the app

Night Owl is the theme in use, and the settings window is open on it with no unsaved edits, when night-owl.yamv-theme.json is deleted in Explorer. A rename outside the app is the same to the watcher: the old id is gone and a new id appears in Yours. Document window 760 × 540 and settings window 980 × 640, side by side, shown at 82%.
