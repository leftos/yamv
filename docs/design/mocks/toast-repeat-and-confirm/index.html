Repeat · The same failure toasts again while its card is still up

Window 700 × 420, shown at 60%; details at 125%. "The same" here means the same text; a different failure always gets its own card. The thin line at a card's foot is the time left of its 5 s. Windows repeats a held key about 30 times a second, so a held Ctrl+] with widths.json locked fails about 45 times in 1.5 s.
